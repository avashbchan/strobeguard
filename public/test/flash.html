⚠ Photosensitivity warning

This page shows intentionally flashing patterns, including fast full-screen and red flashes, for testing StrobeGuard. They can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy and can cause migraines or discomfort.

Do not continue if you are photosensitive. Keep the window small, keep the room lit, and look away from it while testing. Watch StrobeGuard's output and meters, not this page.

Patterns start only after you click one. The page opens on a steady gray screen.
